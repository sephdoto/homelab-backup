Homelab Backup

A personal backup workflow using rclone to store encrypted backups in the owner's Google Drive account.

What it does

Homelab Backup creates backups of self-hosted applications, including Vaultwarden and Paperless-ngx. Backup archives are encrypted locally with rclone crypt before they are uploaded to Google Drive. The Google Drive connection is used for backup upload, retention cleanup, listing, and recovery.

Who can use it?

This is a private, non-commercial tool for its operator. It does not offer sign-ups or a public backup service.
